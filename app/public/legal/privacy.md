# 1. Overview

10X.MEME is operated by **Code Hunt Pty Ltd, trading as 10X Meme** (“10X”, “10X.MEME”, “we”, “us” or “our”), an Australian company.

We operate 10X.MEME websites, Farcaster Mini Apps, Discord communities and bots, blockchain and NFT experiences, waitlists, APIs, tools and related services (collectively, the **“Services”**).

Our Services combine information from social protocols, public blockchains, NFT marketplaces and other third-party services to provide identity, community, discovery, verification, eligibility, transaction and other crypto-related functionality.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, where it comes from, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights available to you.

**We do not ask for or store your private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords or Discord passwords.**

Where you interact with a blockchain or marketplace through 10X, transactions and orders may require authorization or signing through your own wallet.

# 3. Information We Collect

### A. Farcaster & Social Data

When you use a 10X Farcaster Mini App or other Farcaster-connected feature, we may receive information made available through the Farcaster Mini App context, Farcaster protocol or related APIs.

This may include:

* Farcaster ID (“FID”);
* username and display name;
* profile picture and bio;
* public Farcaster profile information;
* account and profile metadata;
* follower and following information;
* public social graph information;
* public casts, reactions and other Farcaster activity;
* verified or associated wallet addresses;
* the Farcaster client or context through which you access a Mini App; and
* identifiers and metadata associated with your interaction with a Mini App.

We may use services such as **Neynar** to enrich or “hydrate” this information with additional publicly available or derived Farcaster information.

Depending on the features we use, Neynar data may include account metrics, social graph information, verified addresses, user or quality scores, activity data and other information associated with a Farcaster identity.

Although much of this information is publicly available, we may treat it as personal information where it relates to, identifies, or can reasonably be linked with an individual.

# 7. Blockchain Data Is Public & Immutable

Blockchain networks are decentralized systems that are not controlled by 10X.

When a transaction is broadcast to a public blockchain, information such as your wallet address, transaction details, asset movements and transaction hash may become permanently and publicly accessible.

10X generally cannot:

* modify blockchain records;
* erase a transaction from a blockchain;
* delete information independently stored by blockchain nodes; or
* prevent third parties from independently obtaining public blockchain information.

If you request deletion of information from 10X, we may be able to delete or de-link information stored in systems we control, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

This does **not** delete the underlying information from a public blockchain or from third-party systems outside our control.
