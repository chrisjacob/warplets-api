# 10X product roadmap

Updated 25 November 2026. Governed by the [brand alignment decision](BRAND_ALIGNMENT_DECISION_2026-11-24.md); the executable presentation lives in [public-content.mjs](../site/public-content.mjs).

| Phase | Product scope | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Product hierarchy, terminology, $10X theme and app-local UX | Local implementation; not a token launch |
| Season 0 standalone beta | First 10X Meme: $10X; 1,000 Cards; planned US$1 beta mint; separate paired Attention Token where ready | Not scheduled; paired campaign start/end unconfirmed |
| Beta learnings | Participation, comprehension, economics, operations and release readiness | Required before expansion |
| Monthly originals and Cards | Target one new 10X Meme and 1,000-card edition per month; intended US$10 standard mint under approved configuration | Planned; Season 1 unscheduled |
| Daily Attention Tokens | Up to one daily spotlight, with skips/extensions and overlapping reward campaigns | Planned; no start date |
| Permanent 10X NFT | One 10,000 collection with published Level-based rules | Planned; timing follows dependencies, not assumed simultaneous with beta |

Post-beta phases need not follow table order. No countdown or automatic activation is approved.

## Follow-up queue

1. Confirm beta scope, separate paired-token identity and approved campaign dates/timezone.
2. Decide original supply split and designer allocation; keep CEX reserve, discounted OTC and listing-linked burns excluded.
3. Apply the adopted exponential brand architecture; confirm Cards naming and final collectible/social artwork separately.
4. Prepare reviewed US$1 beta mint/payment configuration without changing historic SOL terms. Confirm normal US$10 release configuration separately.
5. Resolve the existing custody, funding, provider, wallet, legal and release gates for capabilities actually offered in beta.
6. Run a short comprehension review: can visitors distinguish $10X, its paired Attention Token, the permanent collection and Cards? Can they identify what is available now?
7. Schedule later launches only after readiness and beta learnings. Reuse existing analytics if available; no new tracking service is part of this work.

The [engineering blueprint](../blueprint/README.md) remains the implementation backlog. Its dated economic evidence and test history are retained; use this roadmap for the current product hierarchy and launch sequence.
