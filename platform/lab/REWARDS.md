# Earn and NFT Vault distributor check

The [Foundation Rewards program](https://explorer.solana.com/address/REWArDioXgQJ2fZKkfu9LCLjQfRwYWVVfsvcsR5hoXi) was executable on mainnet at finalized slot **465655227**. The finalized programdata account reported deployment at slot **422571636** and an upgrade authority. Its allocated executable digest and source references are in `fixtures/rewards-program.json`. [Inspected source](https://github.com/solana-foundation/rewards/tree/79d5a3be1fed12021feb8d4ecd909b0fbc15c425) · [Audited-through commit and scope](https://github.com/solana-foundation/rewards/blob/79d5a3be1fed12021feb8d4ecd909b0fbc15c425/audits/AUDIT_STATUS.md)

The proposed Earn configuration funds a Merkle distribution with revocation disabled, a linear schedule beginning **24 hours after** market activation and ending 24 hours later, and the maximum signed 64-bit clawback timestamp. The latter makes closure unreachable at practical cluster times, although the wire format contains a finite timestamp rather than a native forever flag. A zero clawback timestamp would let the authority close immediately in the inspected source. The production gate must verify the root, exact funding, flags, timestamp, upgrade authority, cost and correspondence between reviewed source and deployed binary. Users need independent, durable access to proofs. [Merkle close logic](https://github.com/solana-foundation/rewards/blob/79d5a3be1fed12021feb8d4ecd909b0fbc15c425/program/src/instructions/merkle/close_distribution/processor.rs) · [Program vesting schedules](https://github.com/solana-foundation/rewards/blob/79d5a3be1fed12021feb8d4ecd909b0fbc15c425/program/src/utils/vesting_utils.rs)

The replay uses public bytecode in an offline VM with local keys. It tests zero release at the cliff, partial claims at hour 36, an unauthorized early close, a full revoke attempt and a day-800 claim. A separate ownership scenario transfers a one-of-one SPL asset to a second wallet. The underlying Merkle leaf binds the original reward wallet; the claim instructions accept no current NFT-owner evidence. This is suitable to evaluate for a wallet-based Earn award. The live NFT-owner requirement needs an additional on-chain custody mechanism. [Claim account and proof handling](https://github.com/solana-foundation/rewards/blob/79d5a3be1fed12021feb8d4ecd909b0fbc15c425/program/src/instructions/merkle/claim/processor.rs)

[The captured program replay](https://github.com/chrisjacob/warplets-api/actions/runs/42004141175) confirmed those vesting, close and revoke tests. Alice claimed 50 at hour 36 and a remaining 50 at day 800 after selling the test NFT. Bob's claim failed. See [Core signer composition](CORE-VAULT.md) for the separate NFT-bound claimant experiment.

On the same isolated Linux reference environment as the Meteora and Kamino checks, run `node platform/lab/fetch-rewards-replay-elf.mjs /tmp/rewards.so` and `node platform/lab/replay-rewards.mjs /path/to/october-reference /tmp/rewards.so`. Any unknown deployment change, proof failure, discrepancy in balances or unexpected authority success must fail the gate.
