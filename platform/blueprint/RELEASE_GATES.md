# Financial and production release gates

**Every R stage is outside overnight authority.** B-task completion authorizes neither funds nor deployment. All B work remains local: no remote Cloudflare writes/resource provisioning, signed transactions, service purchase, token funding, broadcaster or new authority/security probes. Optional observations use public, free, uncredentialed reads only. Missing access stays missing and does not prevent ordinary offline implementation.

This register specifies evidence and decisions for a later separately authorized release review. It is not a queue to start when B tasks finish. Production deployment remains prohibited until the founder explicitly authorizes a concrete reviewed release. No standing threshold, proposed actor or approval field below grants that authorization.

## Evidence and decision rules

Every gate packet identifies requirement IDs, exact code/policy/program/configuration versions, evidence digest/time/scope, actor, costs, limitations and invalidation triggers. Distinguish a local arithmetic pass, captured-program behavior, read-only deployed observation, reviewed source/binary correspondence and actual funded outcome. They are different evidence classes. Missing or incompatible requirements cannot be changed to pass by a configuration flag.

The founder decides product tradeoffs, prices, commitments and cash spending. A qualified independent reviewer assesses custody/authority and relevant financial implementation. Selected public programs retain their own upgrade authorities; record that trust explicitly. The application operator may prepare records and monitor an approved action. A scoped wallet signer approves only the exact reviewed message. No agent receives private keys or autonomous financial authority through this document.

## R01 — Permanent, transferable NFT claims

**Unlock evidence.** Exact reserve and claim formats must economically back all 10,000 Genesis positions from the first production ATT; applicable active Season reserves also participate. Preserve the original launch clock: zero through 24 hours, linear through hour 48; minting and transfer do not restart it. Frozen Season snapshots and already-claimed units cannot be rewritten. Demonstrate current-owner control, permanent remaining liabilities and portable independent proofs/recovery for the selected implementation.

The existing Core asset-signer/Rewards fixture is encouraging: Alice claimed 30 of 100 and Bob received 70 after transfer, while Alice was rejected. It does not establish the future asset-identity creation authority, unbiased reserve assignment, every asset/collection plugin, marketplace custody or source/audit coverage. The controlled disposable key in that experiment is not a production reserve system.

**Unresolved compatibility.** No product Burn button/API is settled policy; external burn safety is not solved. Existing evidence shows ordinary burns can strand claims, update authority can remove the experimental veto, and a permanent burn delegate can override it. The unreachable-update-authority variant restricts normal metadata changes. Select a reviewed policy that preserves required Genesis refreshes and Season progression without silently extinguishing liabilities or inserting a central database recovery promise.

Provide DR-V1 custody/permanence, DR-V2 burn/mutation and DR-V3 reserve-assignment records with alternatives, account/state counts, authority powers, historical-claim effects and complete costs. Explain potential Level leakage through public claim amounts/mappings and the exact public reveal definition. Do not delay vested rights merely to maintain an unproven secrecy promise. A practically maximal finite clawback field is not a native forever flag; document its implications and upgrade trust. If no affordable compatible design exists, keep financial launch deferred or seek separately approved review funding.

## R02 — Continuous Stake and indefinite Earn awards

**Stake unlock evidence.** The selected farm must hold exactly 5% gross original supply, deposit/reward the same ATT, accrue over ten days continuously, allow immediate withdraw/re-stake and harvest without withdrawal, and preserve principal and earned-unclaimed rewards after emissions end. Empty periods cannot give the next depositor catch-up rewards. Verify the actual fixed-point rate/rounding and end-of-campaign recovery conditions for the selected supply/decimals.

The normal Kamino fixture reconciled its whole-rate example. Its separate principal counterexample also succeeded: an unrestricted administrator took 10 of 100 staked units. That remains a release failure. Provide an enforceable permission matrix for initialization, curve updates, withdraw authority, vault withdrawal, ended-curve reset and surplus recovery. An operator promise or empty current authority field is insufficient. Immutable setup must still address required end operations. A custom controller or fallback requires a new bounded review/cost case, not an unreviewed fork.

Verify the selected global fee and its change powers. A nonzero third-party charge changes user net, not the 5% supply allocation; present exact amounts and alternatives to the founder. Never top up from another allocation. Recover/burn only demonstrated unused units; specify the remaining open destination policy. Review applicable SDK/program licensing and source/deployed/audit correspondence.

**Earn unlock evidence.** Ratify the currently proposed global UTC board and activation-relative day-eleven vesting schedule before funding. Require exact-cutoff completeness, immutable final awards, per-wallet caps, valid empty versus pending distinction, partial/indefinite claims and no sweep of allocated balances. Verify program-specific roots and vesting parameters. The public archive's SHA-256 integrity proof is not automatically a Foundation Rewards program proof. A wallet-bound Earn award and NFT asset-signer award are separate formats and authority paths. Independently available proofs must survive service loss.

## R03 — Reviewed execution, exact costs and funded operations

**Unlock evidence.** Typed factories must derive every approved instruction/account from verified source state; validate exact message bytes, signers, privileges, lookups, network/genesis, lifetime, output bounds and cost ceiling. Bind the cost packet to the same message, program/configuration and funding-state hashes. Demonstrate recovery for ambiguous outcomes without duplicated funding, mints, claims or burns. Physical supported-wallet behavior and the real provider's receipt/finality semantics must be assessed separately from synthetic browser tests.

Complete costs include account existence/size/rent, protocol charges, network/priority fees, random fulfillment where applicable, retry ceilings, claimant expenses, proof retention, peak upfront cash and refund assumptions. Use fresh sourced conversion and verified available cash. Target below US$10 and within 20% of cash; only a separately approved exception can reach US$30. Above the current ceiling remains local. The 50% reserve is advisory; new recurring services remain capped at US$5/month until revised.

The founder approves a concrete plan, named payer/signer, limits, staffed period and recovery route. A separately bounded protocol experiment must not be marketed as a full ATT campaign. Approval for such an experiment does not authorize production deployment or a later unreviewed message. A real provider, signer or broadcaster is introduced only within the separate approved scope.

## R04 — Paid NFT, fulfillment and marketplace operations

**Unlock evidence.** Enforce finite reserve inventory and unbiased assignment, irreversible payment/draw ordering, timeout/refund behavior without free redraw, ownership at execution, atomic or reviewed escrow progression, and durable result/retry receipts. Preserve same-stage wallet limits and shared inventory; founder/free allocations remain visibly separate from paid demand. All owners retain historical Vault rights through refresh, reroll, upgrade, transfer and expiry.

Select entropy, artwork, marketplace and wallet integrations using concrete compatibility/licensing/cost evidence. The recorded beacon candidate alone does not establish payment ordering, non-withholding, on-chain verifiability or metadata authority. No paid provider is selected automatically. Show cost-plus PFP fulfillment terms, randomness failure/retry costs, rent/refund payer and moderation policy. Apply relevant legal review to paid randomness and public incentives.

The first Genesis whitelist stage remains 0.1 SOL; later prices stay open until founder publication with required notice. Public sale access and founder inventory backstop each require their own explicit decision. Use the confirmed advisory ASAP/7/14/30 vote; rapid sellout does not auto-open a tranche. Free Seasons require revealed Solana Genesis 1X eligibility at the defined snapshot. Marketplace tests must explain any pre-sale owner claim and remaining-balance disclosure; royalties are not guaranteed cash.

## R05 — One staffed funded ATT pilot

**Unlock evidence.** The first production ATT conserves the entire 80/10/5/2.5/2.5 split and has enforceable Earn, Stake and Vault custody, including all unminted Genesis reserves. When no Season is active, its full 5% Vault portion goes to Genesis permanently. Deferring a dashboard or public NFT sale does not defer these obligations. R04 paid NFT release can wait only if R01 already guarantees fair future assignment and claim rights.

Revalidate exact market activation, 90-to-1 fee behavior, supported mint behavior, pool bounds, permanent LP lock, collectible fee rights, current program/configuration and credible OG pricing. Finalize the legitimately empty bootstrap board and burn only the first 1% Earn budget; missing observations remain pending. Do not use founder token inventory, nominal FDV or unreconciled volume as cash revenue.

The founder chooses and publishes the staffed Sydney window before launch; no morning/evening hour is selected here. Approve at most one initial campaign with reconciled cash, exact message/packet, manual go/hold decision and recovery coverage. Keep at most one actual launch per Sydney date and the ten-campaign capacity guard. Stop new activity on unexplained variance, budget or critical evidence failure while preserving existing claims and withdrawals.

## R06 — Evidence-based phased growth

Scale only from observed reconciled results. The PRD's proposed expansion condition is three consecutive campaign settlements with zero unexplained balance variance, no unresolved critical issue and measured cost within budget. Treat that as a founder-reviewed operating gate, not automatic launch permission.

Open Genesis tranches after their R04 conditions, then Seasons after expiry snapshots, free eligibility, two-NFT progression, reroll persistence and historical claims pass their additional gates. Later Seasons never redirect old Genesis fallback allocations. Monitor moderator throughput, participant costs, account resources, cash reserve liabilities and actual net receipts. Optional Mini Apps, native marketplace execution, AI moderation and other chains require separate demand/cost cases. Each funded expansion and any production deployment needs its own explicit approved scope.
