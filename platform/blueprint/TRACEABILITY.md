## Coverage beyond individual tests

- Solana-first ATT/OG curation, Vault → Stake → Earn navigation and preserved legacy communities: B09/B10, B14/B15, B19, R05/R06.
- API-first, optional Mini Apps and independent clients: B01/B02/B06/B09/B11/B12/B15; no Farcaster prerequisite for core use.
- Founder curation, admission capacity, final moderation and operator accountability: B05/B06/B12/B13. One thousand synthetic contributors is a machine-load scenario, not a claim that one moderator can review their content.
- Paid mints/upgrades/rerolls and Genesis owner-reference image refresh: B10/B11/B15/B17 produce local workflows; actual payment, generation and fair fulfillment remain R04.
- Security boundaries and permanent financial rights: B02/B03/B08/B12/B15/B19 plus R01–R05. Existing authority incompatibilities are release failures, never waived by UI changes.
- Budget and phased delivery: B13/B16/B19/B20, R05/R06. The first public ATT must fund every allocation even when NFT sales open later.

## Evidence states

Use not-run, local-passed, captured-vector-passed, measured-read-only, manual-required, failed, or release-approved-for-named-scope. Preserve an actual failed safety finding even when normal-path fixture tests pass. Never turn missing provider data, physical-device evidence, audit review or spend approval into a test skip counted as product acceptance.
