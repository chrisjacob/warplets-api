# B19 — Financial evidence and permanent-claim dossier

**Dependencies:** B08, B11 and B16. **No dependency on B17.** **Mode:** strictly local.

## Outcome and requirements

Produce a machine-readable financial evidence index, portable Vault commitment/recovery artifacts, program-specific claim-format checks using existing vectors, and concrete custody/farm decision dossiers. Consume unselected metadata/identity contracts as unresolved inputs. B19 completes when the local artifacts and limitations are verifiable; it does not require choosing custody, randomness or a metadata provider.

Preserve permanent historical claims, complete unminted backing, original launch clocks and current-owner authority. The first funded ATT requires its complete promised allocation/custody system even if public NFT sales happen later. Preserve the proposed UTC/day-eleven Earn mapping locally and flag it for release ratification. A successful fixture never clears an incompatible permission model.

## Ordered implementation

1. Define gate records with requirement IDs, evidence kind, source commit/digest, program/configuration, network/slot/time, applicability, limitations and review owner. Distinguish missing, fixture-proven, incompatible, review-required and accepted-for-named-scope. No record enables execution.
2. Export compact Vault manifests referencing the immutable full inventory commitment, original supply/budgets, Season launch snapshots, reserve coordinates, observed asset bindings and cumulative claims. Include every unminted obligation without requiring 10,000 writes per launch. Separate public commitments from private entropy/ownership evidence.
3. Verify pinned digests and restore into an empty local database. Reproduce unminted reserve accrual without claim authority before binding, then claims at hour 36, after ownership transfer and after Season expiry. Conflicting bindings/claims hold; restoration never recreates spent entitlement.
4. Specify wallet-bound Earn versus stable asset-signer Vault claimant formats. Existing public SHA-256 archive proofs are not the Rewards program's Merkle format. Add pure codecs only where existing inspected vectors support them; identify distribution/mint, amount, vesting, root, claimant type and policy. Missing matching vectors remain missing. Do not execute programs or generate new transaction evidence.
5. Produce reserve-assignment and asset-policy decision records. The controlled future asset key in the old fixture did not prove fair mint assignment. No burn button does not prevent external burns. Existing Oracle removal/permanent-delegate findings remain incompatible; unreachable update authority restricts metadata/progression. Account for public claim amounts revealing Level before a planned reveal. Do not resolve that tension by withholding vested rights or fabricating secrecy.
6. Produce farm authority/precision reports. The existing unrestricted-admin principal counterexample remains a failed release condition. Enumerate required upkeep versus forbidden principal access. Preserve exactly 5% gross funding; a measured nonzero fee needs explicit net disclosure, never another budget's top-up. Idle recovery excludes principal and earned-unclaimed rewards. Whole-rate execution does not establish fractional precision parity.
