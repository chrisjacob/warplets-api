## Ordered implementation

1. Freeze named commands: publish stage, freeze eligibility, reserve operation, apply observation, cast vote and record final tranche decision. Each carries principal, business identity, request digest/key, expected revisions and explicit clock. Snapshot cut remains published `stage.opensAt`.
2. Load bounded input/revisions, compute outside storage, then use B02's guarded batch: fresh attempt nonce, conditional receipt claim, every child write gated to that claim, final aborting count/invariant assertion. Scope/ownership/limits/versions must be rechecked atomically. Measure serialized inventory size before choosing normalization; never expose staged half-results.
3. Add immutable progression quotes binding principal, kind, collection, target/material IDs and versions, policy, exact fee, total-cost ceiling, expiry and outcome digest. Commands carry `quoteId`, binding digest, versions, reviewed ceiling and expiry. Atomically verify the stored quote and all current predicates before reserving both assets. Resolve accepted identical idempotency replay **before** expiry/pause checks; changed-body replay conflicts. A new command cannot accept an expired quote or substitute increased costs.
4. Apply mint binding, ownership/Level history, operation receipt and Vault slot changes atomically. Replay retains the same draw and result. An observed external transfer during a local reservation creates an explicit evidence conflict/hold; never claim that a database lock prevented chain movement. Local observations remain fixtures unless their evidence adapter establishes more.
5. Port frozen advisory electorate/votes/decisions, 48-hour timing, four choices, notice and unsold-inventory hold. Count one wallet once even when in both groups. Public-stage/founder approvals remain explicit local records, not spending authority.
6. Wire only tested public/member methods into local Worker routes and capability discovery. Privileged imports, publication and founder decisions stay command-only until B12; a member session cannot authorize them. Keep unsupported reads absent. Fixture allocation counts are not verified sales; all financial execution flags remain false.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Record SQL/parameter/byte limits measured locally, migration/contract diffs, race results and receipt evidence. No remote Cloudflare writes/resources, money, signing, broadcast, deployment or new authority probes. This is local persistence parity, not enforceable custody.

Rollback stops new admissions while retaining reads, accepted receipts, snapshots and pending recovery. Additive migrations remain intact; never delete an unknown operation or reset a draw/counter to recover.
