## Ordered implementation

1. Define separate request/evidence/financial states. Local operations always have `financialState:not-applicable-local`. Represent waiting for prior admission, entropy, fulfillment or conflicted evidence without fabricated paid/submitted/refunded phases.
2. Expose only an opaque public commitment and non-sensitive status before authorized reveal. Withhold outcome-reconstructing salt, context, proof, round, admission order and combinations derivable from timestamps/identifiers, including owner receipts. Internal evidence remains intact. Publish the replayable transcript only after irreversible assignment and permitted reveal; never disclose future slot mappings.
3. Use existing recorded beacon proof verification for opted-in fixtures. Bind original admission and same-result retry; manual preferred tickets remain forbidden for protected inventory. Candidate status remains unselected. Do not automate paid timeout/cancel/refund: selective abort and withholding policy require a concrete payment/custody design later.
4. Encode metadata permissions: immutable asset/slot/collection and Genesis Level; Season Level only through accepted progression; original Season start immutable; expiry derived; historical allocations never mutable; reroll count survives transfer; image authority cannot change any economic field.
5. Implement local Genesis refresh proposal `draft → reference-validated → moderation-pending → preview-ready → owner-approved → fulfillment-pending → fulfilled`, with explicit rejected/failed/owner-changed cases. Reference image is input, never an arbitrary final replacement. Bind owner/version, preview hash and quote; retain all revisions and provider job identity. Recheck current owner and asset version at fulfillment, including transfer after approval; hold instead of applying the previous owner's approval. A supplied fixture preview is labeled as such, not claimed AI generation. No provider/payment makes fulfillment unavailable rather than fake-successful.
6. Prepare a permission/recovery packet for later custody review. Current Core burn/update-authority incompatibilities remain unresolved. Do not configure authority/plugin changes or treat an unsigned B15 plan as permission to sign/submit.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Record state diagrams, permission matrix, transcript/redaction cases, exact proof fixture digests and gates. No spending, remote Cloudflare writes/resources, real-wallet/transaction signing, broadcast or deployment. Production fairness additionally needs trusted payment/order, slot authority and nonselective recovery.

Rollback pauses new proposals/admissions, preserving commitments, same-round recovery and image history. Never release a lock, choose a new draw or erase an economic liability merely to clear a stalled local request.
