## Fixed experimental policy

`holder-participation-policy-v1` measures a bounded sample, not meaningful population growth or an organic lower bound. Its thresholds are engineering hypotheses, not calibrated safety or economic truth. Freeze its JSON checksum in every report; any later rule change needs a new policy version and prospective assessment.

- Reuse the first/latest and recent full-inventory intervals selected by holder-history v1. Aggregate token accounts by owner, exclude the canonical pool vault, reconcile supply/layout and advancing slots, and retain original archive hashes. No interpolated 24h or creation baseline.
- A raw entrant is absent from the interval's starting positive-balance owner set and present at its endpoint. Unmatched entrants remain acquisition-unknown; an unsolicited transfer is not automatically proven dust.
- Reuse the archived canonical signed-buy parser and matching finalized signature history. Buy slots must follow the starting inventory and be no later than its end; transaction times must also be in the actual interval. Missing/conflicting history, unsigned/noncanonical activity and future source evidence cannot support acquisition.
- Require only sampled buys for that owner in the interval, known positive SOL/WSOL protocol quote transfers and consistent transaction-wide net token acquisition. Count transaction-wide net balances once, even with multiple buy instructions. Mixed unrelated transfers, sampled sells and endpoint balances below the entire sampled net acquisition do not support the retained sample.
- Require at least **10,000,000 native lamports or WSOL raw units (0.01 SOL)** of total supported protocol quote transfers, counted by swap instruction. This is native-unit trade size, not historical USD, total wallet spend or fee-inclusive cost. No dollar conversion or dust verdict is inferred.
- Require at least **1,800 seconds** between the latest supported sampled buy and the endpoint inventory's collector time. Retain the entire sampled acquired token amount at that endpoint. This is endpoint consistency, not continuous retention or proof the same units remained untouched.
- Exclude observed creator roles, external/shared fee payers, multiple sampled buyers in one transaction, supported direct developer/shared funding, and supported existing second-hop creator/common-source links. These exclusions are conservative sample screening, not fraud labels. Ordinary sponsored transactions and shared funding remain counterexamples; absent links never certify independence.
- Partition each raw entrant once in a declared priority order: no sampled buy; missing finalized history; sampled sell; unknown quote; mixed net flow; insufficient endpoint balance; observed creator/link; below native-size threshold; retention interval not mature; supported screened endpoint-retained sample. Unknowns are never silently counted as zero-quality holders. Truncated trade/funding/link outputs hold the sample instead of screening an incomplete favorable subset. Reaching the legacy 100-funding-output bound also holds it, because v1 does not always report whether additional edges were truncated.
- A missing/conflicting/invalid latest wallet receipt never falls back to an older favorable sample. The existing source cutoffs, byte/layout/archive limits and private replay remain. Stale descriptive values show their dates; endpoint freshness is 30 minutes and wallet freshness one hour. Fresh collection does not refresh an old owner interval.
- Population trade coverage, beneficial independence, continuous retention, meaningful 24h growth and organic demand remain unknown. No score, rank, eligibility, election or action credit. Sampling classifications are not public token safety verdicts.
