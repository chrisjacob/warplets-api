# 144 — Linked candidate and cohort readiness

22 October 2026. Extends [143's captured cohort recovery](143-durable-post-commit-cohort-recovery.md) by closing the candidate-save producer-commit-to-cohort-readiness gap for the private opt-in path. Holder-backed and receipt-free candidate saves with `cohortPublicationId` now retain their original cohort work before the producer can commit. D1 remains the application backend; wallet snapshot integration and automatic scheduler resumption remain separate work.

## Durable order and transaction checks

`publishLinkedCandidateBatch` stages the original holder receipt when present and constructs the exact original producer plan with an optional `cohort_id`. It previews only that plan's original candidate upsert in a fenced, rolled-back local SQLite transaction. This uses the original `json_set` merge and triggers rather than implementing another version of the merge in JavaScript. It reads the bounded resulting candidate bytes, rolls back every preview effect, and runs the unchanged original `captureCohort` builder on the normalized preview candidate. The original builder retains its maximum-evidence timestamp, policy, daily first-observation behavior and assessment hashes.

The encrypted cohort intent retains the exact embedded producer plan, projected candidate byte hash and original first-row fingerprints. Its readiness is synced **before producer intent readiness and producer SQL**. The producer's retained `cohort_id` binds the companion identity into its own plan hash. Journal publication and reconciliation require the authenticated companion under the same original grant, with the exact embedded parent; missing, corrupt, partial or conflicting companions hold. Native producer publication also requires the matching companion plan, so a direct native call cannot omit the link.

Before a new producer executes, the transaction checks the companion's original first-row fingerprints. After its original ordered SQL and receipt operations, it checks the resulting candidate bytes against the retained projection before recording the publication or committing. A changed merge result rolls back the whole producer, including its receipt reference and ancillary metadata. A competing first observation holds before producer SQL. Spent source allowances and already staged bytes are retained; none are refunded or replaced. A later change after producer commit cannot refresh the original saved cohort during recovery. The separate cohort transaction and original result vectors remain unchanged.

Existing candidate callers without a cohort ID and all original receipt/metadata plans without links retain their previous behavior and byte identities. Recover existing unlinked publication IDs through their original known-ID recovery path; do not retrofit a link under an existing identity or rerun a saved candidate workflow. The encrypted journal version and target schema are unchanged. A linked parent cannot create a replacement post-commit cohort intent when its original readiness is missing. Partial files remain held and retained for investigation.

## Explicit recovery and outcomes

`recoverLinkedCandidateBatch` accepts only the retained cohort ID plus `publish` or `reconcile`, alongside the existing private context and current grant. Under the exact original grant, `publish` may materialize a completely absent parent intent from the authenticated embedded original plan, then publish/reconcile that parent and publish/reconcile the original cohort. An existing partial or conflicting parent directory is never repaired or overwritten. This covers a process stop after cohort readiness but before parent readiness, before producer SQL, and after producer commit but before cohort capture/acknowledgement. It needs no original candidate, statements, scheduler selection or source response.

`reconcile` performs read-only checks of both already committed publications; it never falls back to executing pending work or creating a missing intent. The current owner may reconcile committed history after transfer, but cannot materialize or execute the former owner's pending work. Historical publication results grant no lease, source dispatch, quota refund, action authority or evidence qualification.

Inspect `state`, `producer`, `cohort` and any `failure`, as well as the existing candidate caller's producer/cohort/post_commit outcomes. A parent may be committed while the cohort is unconfirmed; a failed reconciliation must preserve that historical parent result. `completed` confirms both original publications; a held result does not imply the producer rolled back. Recovery never repeats the collector or scheduler.
