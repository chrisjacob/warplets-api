# Participation and trading policy — publication draft

Status: draft for owner review, not a published legal notice or an approved production configuration. This does not amend `local-v1`, recalculate an entitlement or decide reserve beneficiaries.

The public explanation should remain short: **Connect → vote → follow selection → claim.** Trading is optional and does not change reward points. Voting is advisory; the operator chooses one candidate or skips. Show the applicable versioned rules, voting cutoff, actual launch time, vesting/release schedule and claim deadline where relevant. Never present finalized/unfunded rewards as available funds.

Verified ATTN trading ranks are public by default and use realized net USD P&L, moving-average purchase cost and disclosed fees. Supported off-app swaps count only with complete accepted transaction and independent valuation evidence. Rewards, deposits and self-transfers do not create trading profits. Per-OG results remain separate by mint. Public snapshots and shared cards retain their as-of time, window, simulated/observed status and source root. Corrections create new versions; existing shared results remain historical records.

Before public participation, the owner must approve the precise Sybil/qualification rules, evidence required for an abuse finding, the person responsible, a reporting channel, dispute window and response target. Any disqualification must name evidence, affected round and applicable rules. Proposed disputes should pause affected pending finalization for review; historical finalized entitlements must never be silently rewritten. Chain transfers and funded direct claims cannot be reversed or paused by hiding a button.

Allocation remainders and empty/unqualified-round reserves remain explicit in immutable allocation records. They are excluded from participant funding until an approved reserve policy identifies custody, beneficiaries, timing, decision authority and recovery. The selected Jito clawback policy remains the approved 365-day rule with native Solana 2-of-3 admin/receiver custody; actual addresses and recovery exercises remain required. EVM treasury uses the owner's Splits preference when an EVM treasury is needed. No bridge is implied.

Notification permission is separate from participation. Default opt-out, topics, three-event daily cap, quiet retry behavior and removal/disable handling must be explained in the live registration flow. No external sharing reward or automatic social posting exists.

Release review must reconcile the final wording with existing shared terms/privacy documents and record the actual publication date. Do not modify another product's client, analytics decision or legal anchors as a side effect of launchpad readiness work.
