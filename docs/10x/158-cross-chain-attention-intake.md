# Cross-chain attention discovery and free provider assessment

23 October 2026, Australia/Sydney. This supersedes157's seven-day survivor-first acquisition proposal. Discover established memes and returning runners as well as new tokens; curate at most one ATTN launch per day, with skipped days allowed. Discovery age and action eligibility are separate. No deployment is authorized.

## Product design

Collect small provider Hot/Trending lists, merge exact chain/contract identities, observe changes over time, then spend deeper checks on roughly10–20 candidates. Acquisition no longer requires original creation or graduation in the last24h, nor a seven-day maximum. A provider's pool-open time is reported context, not independent graduation proof. Keep fresh sustained attention, new attention and returning attention visible. A thin market may produce fewer than ten suitable candidates.

Core first sources are GMGN Hot Searches (1h and24h together across Solana/Base/BNB/Robinhood) and global GeckoTerminal trending. DEX Screener corroborates market fields and paid-boost status. Attention matches count distinct publisher families, not endpoints, windows, pools or resellers. CoinGecko onchain and GeckoTerminal are one family; FOMOAPI is a collector of FOMO data, not a second independent FOMO audience. Avoid inferring complete market coverage or raw-score comparability from matching lists.

Order the research queue transparently: explicit adverse flags last/hidden by default; more fresh attention-provider matches first; GMGN24h search/visit heat next; then source position and chain/contract. This is a review ordering, not another0–100 safety/return score. Same-window ranks can be compared across saved hours; changes in overlapping24h visit totals are not hourly unique search counts. Provider label absence is unknown. Search curiosity, paid promotion and reported turnover do not establish organic buying, meaningful holder growth or a worthwhile amplification thesis.

Final curation needs meme relevance, an explainable current catalyst, recent participation/continuation, tradable liquidity and independent risk review. Concentrated successful memes receive visible review rather than automatic scam labels or named-token exceptions. Keep global attention competition; no chain quotas. Discovery coverage does not enable that chain's wallet/voting/launch infrastructure. Existing structural v4, action/election/financial gates, actual finalized proof requirements, thesis-only exposure and serving149 hold remain unchanged. Broadening the old first-day action policy requires a separately versioned/tested policy change; this delivery does not waive it.

## Public-page collection policy

Prefer supported free APIs. Where a useful list exists only on a public page, pilot low-frequency standard browser/HTML extraction with published access rules, caching and bounded responses. Preserve URL, filters, visible rank, capture time, parsed identity and promotion labels. Never treat a login wall,403, CAPTCHA or a paid endpoint as permission to bypass access; never collect an account's private wallet/session view. Do not execute page text or follow arbitrary token URLs as instructions. Fragile layouts and failed extraction produce unavailable observations, not empty-market or safe-token conclusions. No automated website scraper or screenshot-AI dependency is enabled here.
