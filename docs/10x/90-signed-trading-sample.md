## Product behaviour

The three-token comparison now includes recent **signed trading samples**, up to five minutes, one hour and six hours relative to each saved wallet observation. It shows sampled buy/sell instruction counts, distinct buyers/sellers, wallets observed on both sides, separate native quote totals, buyers with a single sampled buy of at least 0.01 SOL, and the largest same-side equal known quote group (instructions/wallets). Actual windows, stale status and evidence references accompany the numbers.

This is descriptive participation evidence. It does not measure independent people, full-market volume, continuous coverage, authentic attention or a wash-trading verdict. Repeated sizes and buying/selling overlap also occur in ordinary order execution and arbitrage. No concern threshold or additional score ceiling is introduced. Large strategic/KOL allocations and later price success receive no exemption or automatic adverse label.

## Fixed experimental policy and accounting

`trade-sample-policy-v1` was fixed before reviewing the new cached comparisons. Checksum: `517c0958f936549941e38886c702d26189fb67e81bb2d1b1e7ae872ea1dd7daa`. These are engineering measurement rules, not calibrated economic thresholds or a preregistered model-promotion study.

- Reuse the latest cutoff-bound archived wallet evidence already replayed for holder acquisition/participation. The shared replay validates input/raw/evidence hashes, exact mint/canonical asset binding, source dates, transaction identity, supported signed trade instructions and existing origin/account rules. The holder and trading projections share one context; no duplicate database read or source replay is added to the normal comparison path. The original holder-participation function and report/checksum remain reproducible.
- Windows are inclusive at both ends and anchored to the saved wallet observation, not current time. Where verified creation is later than the requested start, clip to creation and disclose the shorter actual interval. Future trades, trades before verified creation, future creation, truncated/at-bound trade output and unmatched finalized history hold the affected projection. No later source is backfilled into an earlier cutoff. Missing wallets, rejected evidence and synthetic mode remain explicit non-measurements.
- Count certified instructions once through the existing signature/instruction deduplication. A trade must match an available saved transaction and consistent finalized history via the existing acquisition check. This is source replay, not independent finality attestation. Counts describe the saved sample only; missing/unsupported transactions remain gaps. An empty saved interval can report zero sampled instructions without implying zero market trading.
- Keep buy and sell quote transfers separate. Raw SOL/WSOL 1e9-unit quantities use BigInt throughout summation and decimal display. No float precision loss, absolute-value conversion, buy/sell netting, event-field substitution, inferred USD or total-wallet-cost claim. Missing quotes withhold that direction's total; missing buy quotes also withhold the sized-buyer count. The 0.01 SOL condition applies to one supported buy instruction, not accumulated dust, retained holdings or independent demand.
- Equal known quote groups use exact raw size and side, with wallet breadth shown separately. Unknown amounts cannot join a group. Distinct buyers/sellers are wallet addresses, including any observed creator/linked actors; they are not certified independent participants. Holder acquisition/retention screening remains separately visible.
- Freshness uses the existing one-hour wallet-sample reference; stale measurements remain dated. Nested windows are not additive and are never extrapolated to 24h. Organic USD volume and independent participant counts stay null; no score, eligibility, election or action rule changes.

## Compatibility and replay

`trade_sample` is an optional additive field in `discovery-comparison-v1`. Its mint, cutoff, source mode, policy checksum and report checksum are verified. Older comparisons without it still verify, as do their original holder/candle projections. A private `{inputs, report}` replay function reconstructs the sample from retained acquisition inputs and rejects mismatches. The API adds no endpoint or source contract; OpenAPI extends only DiscoveryComparison and adds a TradeSample schema, leaving all other schemas and paths unchanged.
