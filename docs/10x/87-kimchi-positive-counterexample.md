## Product correction

- Retire the standalone **reported cap above $10M within 24h** warning on discovery cards/details and its aggregate warning count. Price, cap, age, depth and dated evidence remain visible in their normal fields. This rule was never an action exclusion or score/rank penalty.
- Retain the old diagnostic reader, frozen signal cohorts and replay. Label that cap signal as legacy research, with its false-positive limitation. No historical flag/outcome is relabelled or removed; continued diagnostic retention does not reactivate the warning.
- Preserve all v1–v4 evaluators, scorecards and source/evidence/action gates. V3/v4's separate $5M-within-ten-minutes, same-slot, observed concentration and historical creator-acquisition ceilings remain provisional and unchanged. This correction is not a quiet edit of those frozen rules or a token-specific whitelist.
- Large verified concentration remains a control, free-float and exit-risk observation even when a coin performs well. Strategic/KOL context is relevant evidence, not an automatic exemption. A token can have strong attention, high concentration and unresolved integrity simultaneously.

## Required calibration treatment

1. Keep performance/attention, economic participation, supply/control and integrity evidence as separate assessment dimensions. Retain one public score for now; no new competing safety probability or unsourced weighted score.
2. In the next preregistered model study, include rapid runners and concentrated successful launches alongside concentrated failures, ordinary launches and missing-data cases. Record source provenance, allocation/vesting/lock evidence, public disclosure/recipient behaviour, dispersal, executable depth, independent participation and actual selling where support exists. Reputation or narrative cannot substitute for those measurements.
3. Report false positives and missed opportunities as well as detected concerns. “Strong return” and “no observed rug” are different outcomes; neither establishes independent/organic demand. Do not infer a probability from one winner or ignore the denominator of similar concentrated failures.
4. This founder-selected case is retrospective development evidence. Keep it out of unseen-mint/later-day promotion holdouts and do not backfill later success or strategic intent into earlier scores. Do not tune another sequence of model versions against Kimchi, PONYX or any single named token.
5. Review whether cap/launch/concentration-only ceilings over-penalize independently supported demand in a future version, with an explicit preregistered tradeoff and preserved control-risk disclosure. No automatic ceiling removal, renormalized unknown points or action-gate waiver; promote only after the existing F4 review requirements.
